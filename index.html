Rapstar Sim

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Rapstar Sim does not collect, transmit or sell any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

What changed in this version

This version shows our other games in the store tab. To list them, the app downloads a public file from GitHub Pages. That request carries nothing about you — no account, no save data, no identifier — and nothing else about your privacy changed.

What the app stores

Your career — songs, albums, cash, the whole save file — is written to storage on your own device. We never receive it and have no copy of it.

Backups

Android backs the save up with your other app data if you have Google backup switched on, which is what puts your career back after a reinstall or on a new phone. That is a system feature: the file goes to your own Google account, encrypted, and we can neither read it nor restore it for you. Switch it off in Android Settings if you would rather it stayed only on this device — and either way, the game's Settings screen has a Backup & Restore page that copies your save out as text you keep yourself.

Album artwork you choose

If you set a custom album cover, the image you pick is copied into the app's own storage so the artwork survives your gallery changing. That copy stays on your device. The app reads nothing else from your photos and sends nothing anywhere.

Merch product photos

A photo you pick for a product in your store is copied into the app's own storage the same way, and stays on your device.

Permissions

The app requests network access, and uses it for four things: buying something in the store, restoring a purchase you already made and checking whether a newer version exists, all through Google Play, and downloading the public list of our other games shown in the store tab. There is no server of ours, because we do not run one; the list is a static file on GitHub Pages, which, like any website, sees the address a request came from. Picking an album cover uses the system photo picker, which hands over the one image you selected and grants no access to the rest of your library.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, your name or your billing address — the app is only told that a purchase completed, and it records that on your device. There is no receipt of yours stored anywhere else.

All sales are final

Everything sold in this game is digital content delivered immediately, and it is non-refundable. Energy and Funds are spent inside a save file on your own device, so there is nothing for us to take back and no payment for us to return — we never receive the money and cannot reverse a charge.

Restoring is not a refund. Permanent items — the Starter Pack and Max Energy — come back after a reinstall or on a new phone through Restore Purchases in the shop, at no further cost, as long as you use the same Google account. Consumable items do not come back, because they were already used.

Deleting a career, restarting one, uninstalling the game, losing a device, or changing your mind does not entitle you to a refund. Anything beyond that is between you and Google Play under their own refund policy, and their decision is the one that applies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It contains simulated themes of fame, money and mortality, and an explicit-lyrics toggle that changes labels only.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version adds advertising or in-app purchases — the updated policy will ship with that version and the date above will change. Nothing is applied retroactively to data that never left your device.
